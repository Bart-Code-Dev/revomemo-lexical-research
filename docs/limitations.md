# Limitations

- This repository describes methodology and planned evaluation, not completed confirmatory evidence.
- OEWN does not cover every RevoMemo candidate and is English-centric in its current candidate-inventory role.
- WordNet sense boundaries may differ from pedagogical concept boundaries. A mapping can be a close match, narrower or broader educational concept, partial overlap, or unjustified; these are conceptual possibilities, not official status labels.
- A unique lemma-plus-POS OEWN candidate does not establish intended meaning because of coverage limits, sense-granularity differences, domain mismatch, source-context errors, and teaching-concept granularity.
- Resource errors or omissions can propagate into downstream evidence. External structure can make incorrect evidence more explicit without making it correct.
- Typed grounding can create false confidence if provenance and evidence freshness are ignored.
- Model evaluators and independent generations can share systematic biases; model-based graders are not linguistic ground truth.
- Human review improves accountability but can introduce disagreement, cost, and availability constraints.
- The C-versus-D comparison requires strict substantive information matching; otherwise it cannot isolate representation effects.
- Component-ablation conclusions can depend on whether removed information can be reconstructed from remaining fields.
- A fixed benchmark may not represent every language pair, learner context, or instructional goal.
- Synthetic examples illustrate structure only; they are not a lexical dataset or a substitute for evaluation data.

# Limitations

- This repository describes methodology and planned evaluation, not completed confirmatory evidence.
- Sense boundaries, register, and construction acceptability can remain contestable even under a typed representation.
- Typed grounding can make evidence more explicit without guaranteeing that supplied evidence is correct.
- Independent generations can share systematic model biases.
- Human review improves accountability but can introduce disagreement, cost, and availability constraints.
- A fixed benchmark may not represent every language pair, learner context, or instructional goal.
- Synthetic examples illustrate structure only; they are not a lexical dataset or a substitute for evaluation data.

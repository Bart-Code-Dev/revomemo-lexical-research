# Methodology evolution

## Timeline

**2026-08-19** — Public methodology repository created.

**2026-08-19** — Protocol aligned with the submitted research design.

**2026-08-20** — Before confirmatory preregistration and execution, the methodology was refined after a semantic-context consistency audit, evidence-versioning improvements, and an OEWN 2025 lexical-resource preflight.

## What changed

The hypothesis did not change. The construction and auditability of the grounding layer improved: OEWN 2025 core is now documented as a versioned external candidate-sense inventory; contextual mapping is tied to context identity; evidence can become stale rather than being silently reused; and abstention is explicit when available evidence does not justify a mapping.

The historical lesson is methodological rather than an exposed engineering incident: evidence generated against different context versions must not be treated as disagreement over the same evidence. Context-version disagreement is not model disagreement.

## What did not change

This refinement does not change the primary research question, the 240-case benchmark, language directions, risk categories, A/B/C/D conditions, two-generation rule, 1,920 planned primary outputs, primary endpoint, or 60-case robustness stress test. OEWN is not a fifth experimental condition. It refines how lexical grounding is constructed, versioned, audited, and frozen before generation.

The primary benchmark remains an evaluation of typed grounding against flat mappings, on-demand planning, and information-matched unstructured context—not a test of whether OEWN itself is correct. No fine-tuning is introduced; the design concerns external lexical representation, not model-weight changes.

## Preregistration boundary

These refinements occurred before preregistration and confirmatory execution. Once preregistered, a change to the frozen methodology must be reported as an amendment rather than silently substituted.
